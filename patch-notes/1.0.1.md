- KubeJS item type `amcore:hot_stuff`: heated copies of a base item, one item per heat state (warm, hot, scorching),
  set with `.heat(h => ...)`. Hot items are in the tag `amcore:hot_items`.
- Hot items are named after their base item and heat state, and show their temperature in the tooltip.
- Hot items render with a fire glow, give off light and show a heat thermometer.
- Players carrying hot items get burned (new `hot_touch` damage type), configurable in the common config.
- New `create_metallurgy` mod, shipped in the same jar.
